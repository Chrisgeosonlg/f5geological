Two drilling methods

Support matched to the programme

RC and diamond drilling answer different geological questions. Our support adapts to the method, site conditions and information required.

METHOD / RC

Reverse Circulation

Fast, efficient chip sampling for exploration, resource definition and grade-control programmes.

  • Rig and sample-flow coordination
  • Metre-by-metre sample control
  • Chip logging and reference trays
  • Contamination and recovery monitoring
METHOD / DD

Diamond Drilling

Continuous core recovery for structural, lithological and geotechnical understanding at depth.

  • Core orientation and depth checks
  • Core recovery and RQD monitoring
  • Core-yard workflow coordination
  • Logging, photography and sampling support
