Privacy Policy

This policy explains how F5 Geological Solutions Tanzania Limited handles personal information when you visit our website or contact us.

Information we collect

We may receive information you provide when you contact us, request a quotation, or enquire about our services. This can include your name, email address, telephone number, organisation, project details and the contents of your message.

Our website and hosting providers may also process basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, timestamps and security logs. The consent banner stores your privacy choice in your browser’s local storage.

How we use information

We use personal information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, provide requested services, communicate about projects, maintain website security, diagnose technical problems, improve the website and meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and external services

We may share information with service providers that support our website, communications or business operations where reasonably necessary. These providers are expected to handle information securely and only for the relevant service.

The website may link to third-party websites or load resources from third-party providers. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review them before providing information.

Retention and security

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate business records, or as required by applicable law. We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to provide access to, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate record-keeping needs. You may also object to or request restriction of certain processing.

You can choose Accept or Decline in the site consent banner. That choice is saved locally in your browser. Clearing the site’s local storage will reset the banner.

Children’s privacy

Our services and website are intended for businesses and professionals and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or privacy practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its updated date.
